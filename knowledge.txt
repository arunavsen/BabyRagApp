India's national cricket team is governed by the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI). It is one of the most successful teams in international cricket.

India won the ICC Cricket World Cup in 1983 under the captaincy of Kapil Dev and again in 2011 under Mahendra Singh Dhoni. They also won the ICC T20 World Cup in 2007 and the ICC Champions Trophy in 2013.

Sachin Tendulkar, known as the "Little Master", is considered one of the greatest batsmen in cricket history. He scored 100 international centuries.

Virat Kohli is one of the most successful Indian captains and batsmen in the modern era. He is known for his aggressive style and consistency.

Rohit Sharma is the only player to have scored three double centuries in One Day Internationals (ODIs). He is also known for his calm leadership and explosive batting.

Jasprit Bumrah is a leading fast bowler known for his unique action and accuracy, especially in the death overs.

India has a rich cricketing history with iconic venues like Eden Gardens in Kolkata, Wankhede Stadium in Mumbai, and M. A. Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai.

The Indian Premier League (IPL) is a professional Twenty20 league founded by the BCCI in 2008. It is one of the richest and most-watched sports leagues in the world.

India’s current head coach is Rahul Dravid, a former batting legend nicknamed "The Wall" for his solid technique.

M.S. Dhoni is celebrated for his calm demeanor, leadership, and finishing abilities. He led India to all three major ICC trophies: World Cup (50-over and T20) and Champions Trophy.

India's U-19 cricket team has also been highly successful, producing stars like Virat Kohli, Prithvi Shaw, and Shubman Gill.

India's women's cricket team has gained prominence with players like Mithali Raj, Harmanpreet Kaur, and Smriti Mandhana.

Test cricket in India has seen legends like Anil Kumble, Sunil Gavaskar, and Rahul Dravid, who played crucial roles in shaping India’s dominance in red-ball cricket.

The BCCI is the richest cricket board in the world and has significant influence over global cricket decisions.
